# Mechanics at the table

Every mechanic of Neo Forge 3D in one picture each. All pictures were made in "own graphics only"
mode (`CLASH_FREE_ASSETS=1`): self-drawn card frames, type symbols and mana symbols, no card images
and no third-party symbol sheet. See [DOCUMENTATION.md](DOCUMENTATION.md) for how each one is operated.

## Table

### The table

Creatures in the middle, lands on the left, artifacts and command-zone items (emblem, the Ring, dungeon) on the right in up to two rows. The player's medallion sits above the hand, poison to its left, energy and designations (monarch, initiative, the Ring) to its right.

![The table](bilder/tisch.jpg)

### Mulligan

The opening hand large in front of the camera; who goes first, Mulligan or Keep.

![Mulligan](bilder/mulligan.jpg)
